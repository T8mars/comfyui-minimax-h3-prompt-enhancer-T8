# T8 Character Performance Bible

Compiles one current character's objective, obstacle, stakes, tactics, physical task, voice, and listening behavior into a lightweight acting contract. The node itself makes no LLM request.

The bilingual text inside each field is placeholder guidance only; it is never written to the output. Click a field and replace it with your own content.

Quick reference example:

- Scene objective: Get his sister to hand over the car keys.
- Obstacle and stakes: She refuses; failure means missing the last train.
- Tactics: Probe gently → offer a trade → block the exit (enter one tactic per line).
- Physical task and inertia: Packs a bag while steadying trembling hands.
- Voice lock: Low voice, short phrases, slower under stress.
- Mask-break trigger: The smile drops when their father is mentioned.
- Gaze and listening: Avoids her gaze; looks up at the key jingle.

- Write an objective as an action directed at a scene partner or obstacle, not as an emotion label.
- Use one primary tactic per beat and change it only after failure, new information, or a power shift.
- Downstream compilers use no more than three high-signal observable cue channels per character per beat.
- Physical-task and body-state inertia carry across cuts; the voice lock applies only while the character speaks.
- Supplied dialogue remains verbatim and the contract never authorizes invented dialogue.

Connect one character directly to H3, Seedance 2.0, or Storyboard Pack. For a multi-character scene, first use `T8 Character Performance Bible Stack`: it combines up to eight independent characters, rejects duplicate IDs, and requires downstream shots to keep each character's objective, tactic, and observable cues separate. It adds no paid request.
